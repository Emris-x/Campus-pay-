Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

What we collect

When you sign up, we collect your full name, email address, matric number, and registration number. When you make a payment, we record the fee type, faculty account number, and amount you enter.

What we don't do

CampusPay does not hold your money. For fees processed through CampusPay, we prepare and record the transaction; the payment itself moves directly to the university or faculty account you're paying.

How we use your data

Your data is used to generate receipts, maintain your transaction history, and help your faculty verify your payments. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Contact

Questions? Reach out at support@campuspay.app.
